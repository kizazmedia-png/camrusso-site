Services

Three ways to work together, plus content and hourly support.

Every engagement has a defined scope and a written estimate before any work begins. Most clients start with an audit and grow into ongoing work. Hourly support is available for scoped one-off needs.

Pricing is set at scoping based on site size, complexity, and the competitive landscape. Request a consultation for a written estimate.

Package 01 Fixed-scope engagement

SEO & AI-Visibility Audit

A complete diagnostic of what's holding a site back in both traditional search and AI answer engines — delivered as a prioritized fix list and a live walkthrough call, not a PDF dropped over the fence. This is the best first step for any new engagement, and it's where most retainer relationships begin.

What's included

  • Full technical crawl — indexation, crawlability, schema, Core Web Vitals, broken or missing resources
  • On-page review — titles, meta, headings, internal linking, content gaps
  • AI-visibility check — how the brand currently appears (or doesn't) in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews
  • Prioritized fix roadmap sequenced by business impact and effort
  • A live walkthrough call to talk through findings, ask questions, and decide what to do next

Best for

Sites that have grown faster than their SEO foundation — usually e-commerce or service businesses with 50+ pages, real organic traffic to protect, and a sense that something is structurally off but not which lever to pull first.

Package 02 Monthly retainer

Core SEO Retainer

Ongoing ownership of the organic search channel. SEO is not a one-time fix — rankings require continuous maintenance, fresh content, and active adaptation to algorithm changes. A monthly retainer keeps a site improving instead of slipping.

What's included

  • Ongoing technical maintenance — schema, indexation, Core Web Vitals, error monitoring
  • On-page optimization and internal linking improvements across priority pages each month
  • SEO content production — new pages or articles, scoped to the tier
  • Monthly Search Console reporting with clear, prioritized next steps
  • Direct communication channel during business hours for questions and ad-hoc strategy calls

Best for

Businesses that have already had an audit (mine or someone else's) and want a steady hand executing the work — or sites that depend on organic traffic as a primary commercial channel and need ongoing investment, not one-off projects.

Package 03 Specialty monthly retainer

GEO / AIO Retainer

A dedicated program to position a business to be recognized, cited, and recommended by AI search and large language models — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Most SEO retainers ignore the AI answer layer entirely; this one is built around it.

What's included

  • Entity and Organization schema strategy — building the structured-data foundation LLMs use to recognize a brand as a real entity
  • Content positioning for citation — structured, extractable, authoritative formatting that LLMs prefer to quote from
  • Cross-platform consistency — aligning the brand entity across the web (Wikipedia-style descriptions, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, sameAs links) so AI models trust it
  • Ongoing AI-visibility tracking against a defined set of target queries, with monthly reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews as the AI search landscape shifts

Best for

Brands that already rank well in traditional Google but are invisible inside ChatGPT and Perplexity — or category-defining businesses that want to be the answer LLMs reach for when someone asks about their space.
